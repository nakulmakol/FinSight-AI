RBI/2024-25/98 — Master Direction on Prudential Norms (Sample)

Reserve Bank of India — Department of Regulation

Subject: Prudential norms for banks — asset classification and provisioning

Summary:
Banks shall continue to maintain robust provisioning coverage for standard assets.
MSME and retail portfolios require enhanced monitoring where delinquency trends emerge.

Sector-specific notes:
- Banking: Net interest margins expected to remain stable if repo rate is held.
- IT Services: No direct regulatory constraint; focus on global demand and currency impact.
- Energy: Monitor commodity-linked exposure under revised risk-weight guidelines.

Investor caution:
Retail investors should align new equity purchases with personal liquidity needs and existing sector exposure.
RBI does not provide investment advice; this circular governs regulated entities only.
