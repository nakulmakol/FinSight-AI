SEBI Circular — Framework for Retail Investor Protection (Sample)

Securities and Exchange Board of India

Highlights:
1. Enhanced disclosure requirements for listed entities regarding related-party transactions.
2. Stricter norms on social media influencers providing stock tips without registration.
3. Emphasis on suitability — products must match investor risk profile.

Market conduct:
Listed IT companies including Infosys (INFY), TCS (TCS), and Wipro (WIPRO) must comply with quarterly governance disclosures.
Investors should verify exchange filings before acting on news or social media sentiment.

Risk reminder:
Past performance does not guarantee future returns. Concentrated bets in single stocks increase idiosyncratic risk.

Reference: SEBI/HO/2024/045
