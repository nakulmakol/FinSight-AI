Reserve Bank of India
Date: October 2024

POLICY REPO RATE DECISION

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decided to keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 6.50 percent.

Key observations:
1. CPI inflation is projected at 4.5 percent for Q3 FY25, moderating thereafter.
2. GDP growth for FY25 is projected at 7.2 percent with balanced risks.
3. Liquidity conditions remain adequate; systemic stability is maintained.

Implications for equity investors:
- Rate-sensitive sectors (Banks, NBFCs, Real Estate) may see margin stability in the near term.
- IT and export-oriented sectors benefit from stable rupee volatility assumptions.
- High-growth midcaps remain sensitive to global rate expectations and FII flows.

Guidance for portfolio positioning:
- Maintain diversification across sectors; avoid concentration above 35% in a single sector.
- Consider staggered entry for new positions during policy transition periods.
- Monitor RBI forward guidance on inflation trajectory before increasing leverage.

Effective: Immediate
